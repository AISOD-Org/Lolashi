Sam Tiliindje was born in 1980-08-15 Lubango angola during namibian struggle for independence.

Both mother Tshaalula ya Nuumbosho and father Nande ya Nghilifilwa are veterans of the namibian liberation.
During his early years, him plus other 99 children were sent for school in the then GDR east Germany in a small town called stassfurt were they attended primary school until namibia got independence in 1990 21 march.
After independence they were sent to go to their motherland and reunite with their families where Sam Tiliindje continued with his school first at accacia Secondary school in windhoek before he went to the north and again continued with his primary at both Ehafo and Oshipumbu combined school.
